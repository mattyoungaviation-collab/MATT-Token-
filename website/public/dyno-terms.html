Participation terms

Entry and refunds

Each ticket requires 35 RON and 700,000 MATT, plus network gas. MATT stays in the raffle contract during review. Disqualified and cancelled tickets can claim their original 35 RON and 700,000 MATT. Network gas is not refunded and daily allowance is not restored. Claims do not expire.

Prize delivery

Owner-held on Ethereum: Water Dyno #1358 has no contract escrow. Delivery depends on the owner sending the NFT to the assigned winner. The multisig manually initiates the five RON payouts from reserved raffle funds. Submitted transfers remain pending until verified. An assigned winner is never rerolled because delivery fails.

Contract administration

The multisig and the activated emergency admin can independently upgrade the raffle proxy with no timelock, including during sales, review, randomness and payouts. The selected emergency wallet is 0x1DAb596D0121C250a24B00137E84170FA6874be6. Its key can bypass the 2-of-3 threshold. Upgrades can change active logic, potentially putting all contract-held funds at risk. Live authority must be checked on chain; local configuration is not an executed grant. The contracts are not immutable and this build is not an audit.

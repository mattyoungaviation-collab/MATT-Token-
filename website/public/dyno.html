PLAIN-LANGUAGE TERMS

Know what a ticket means.

Both 35 RON and 700,000 MATT are required. Approval may be a separate transaction. Reverted network gas is still charged.

Discord OAuth verifies an account and server membership—not unique personhood. Each linked account and wallet may buy at most three tickets per UTC day across rounds.

Disqualified and cancelled tickets can claim their original 35 RON and 700,000 MATT. Daily allowance is not restored.

Odds with k tickets are k/1,000 per draw. Across six independently derived streams, chance of at least one assignment is 1 − (1 − k/1,000)⁶.

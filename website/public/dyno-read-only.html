How the round is planned to work

One ticket requires 35 RON + 700,000 MATT, plus network gas. Ticket MATT is held by the raffle contract, not sold at checkout. A disqualified or cancelled ticket can claim its original RON and MATT. Sales have no deadline; exactly 1,000 eligible tickets are needed before review and draws.

Each ticket is eligible in all six draws, including after a win. The NFT stays with its Ethereum owner until manually sent to the assigned winner. Five RON payouts are initiated separately by the multisig.

KNOW BEFORE YOU ENTER

THE IMPORTANT RULES.

NO SALES DEADLINE

The round progresses after 1,000 eligible tickets, review, a frozen snapshot, and accepted Ronin VRF randomness.

REPEAT WINS ALLOWED

A ticket remains active for every draw. With one ticket, the chance is 1/1,000 in each draw.

DUAL-ASSET REFUNDS

A disqualified or cancelled ticket keeps a claim for its original 35 RON and 700,000 MATT. Gas is not refunded.

CLAIMS DO NOT EXPIRE

Unpaid RON prizes and eligible refunds remain liabilities until successfully paid.

MANUAL NFT DELIVERY

#1358 remains with the owner on Ethereum until it is sent to the assigned winner. There is no NFT contract escrow.

CONTRACTS ARE UPGRADEABLE

The disclosed multisig and activated emergency admin have immediate upgrade authority. Review the complete trust disclosure before entry.

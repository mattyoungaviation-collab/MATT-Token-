## Completed locally

`ProjectDynoKatanaAggregateAdapter` implements the official AggregateRouter V3 exact-input command with a fixed single-pool MATT/WRON path and fee. It transfers input to the router atomically with `payerIsUser=false`, avoiding router/Permit2 approvals. Checks cover exact intake, complete spending, measured output, unwrap receipts and final MATT delivery. Partial fills revert. Reentrancy protection and nonzero minimum output remain enforced. No arbitrary commands, paths, sweep or allow-revert flag are accepted. The legacy `exactInputSingle` adapter remains available only for an explicitly verified legacy router. Neither source compatibility nor mock checks identify the live router or establish bytecode equivalence.

`fork-katana.js` runs only on an ephemeral Hardhat fork with a private read-only source RPC, pinned block and explicit route manifest. It checks canonical pool/fee, the one-hour guard, real wrap/unwrap, three sequential 700,000-MATT sales and an illustrative 136.5672-RON buy. Its bootstrap trade uses low output protection on the local fork only, not a production policy. The longer capacity probe above stopped at sale 53 even with one-hour spacing and the maximum allowed sale tolerance. Manipulation simulations and a reviewed production liquidity floor remain outstanding.

`preflight-network.js` checks official Ronin 2020 or Saigon 202601 chain identity, coordinator code, exact fee, pinned block/hash and canonical finalized-tag support. Callback gas is a diagnostic default, not measured acceptance evidence. The selected 25-RON operations budget is compared with an actual fee only after a successful call. A fee quote does not prove delivery or gas adequacy. Missing finality never falls back to latest; fee failure never substitutes an estimate or provider seed.

`prepare-deployment.js` has no provider, signer, private key or broadcast path. Complete reviewed inputs produce seven ordered CREATE drafts: adapter, guard, both implementations, both ERC1967 proxies, and the immutable VRF refund receiver. Initialization is atomic and binds predicted counterpart addresses. Separate multisig drafts bind the refund receiver, grant the backup keeper role and create a configured draft round. Opening and funding are not included. Incomplete inputs produce blockers and no transaction calldata. Predictions must never be presented as deployed contracts.

The production example now includes reviewer, separate primary/backup keepers, router integration, factory/fee, callback policy and terms hash. These remain owner-approved public configuration, not invented accounts. Identity/keeper wallets cannot reuse the treasury multisig; primary/backup keepers must be distinct. Service rollout, monitoring, nonce replacement and backup takeover remain unfinished. Private RPCs and wallet credentials belong in hosting secrets, not the manifest, chat or reports.
